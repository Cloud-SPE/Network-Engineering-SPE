This is the picture to remember. On top, the apps: John's desk talks to builder core over REST, ChatLPT imports it, and your app can do either. In the middle, builder core. Underneath, the Livepeer stack that already exists, which we did not replace: the Python SDK calls an orchestrator's runner, asking the remote signer (livepeer/node) to sign each payment; the signer charges the actor's allocation in Clearinghouse Batteries. The orange dashed line is the part builder core adds on the way back: it reads Batteries' usage feed and matches every charge to the job that caused it, which is what makes per-job cost and reconciliation possible.
